Why this works

  • Not a race — a sweep. Liquidatable orders sit for hours to days. Checking every few minutes beats bots that check daily.
  • Simulate before firing. Every shot is confirmed via on-chain simulation first — reward, energy cost, and net profit known in advance.
  • Rent energy on demand. ~5–13 TRX rent per shot vs 20–460 TRX rewards. No stake needed to start; profits compound toward one.
  • Hard guardrails. feeLimit=0 (never burn TRX on a failed race), net-profit gate before every fire, paper mode by default.
